SHEET THEME: PAINTED FLOOR MARKINGS, NOT OBJECTS. Each item is a flat, worn, scratched paint marking on its own small square or rectangular patch of DARK GREY metal floor (the patch is part of the item, so its edges will blend into a dark floor): yellow and black hazard stripe bands, red diagonal hatching zones, yellow chevron arrows, white and yellow circle landing-pad markings, outlined boxes with corner brackets, dashed lane lines, stencilled two-character sector codes, red no-entry stripes, a drain grate, a riveted floor access plate. Completely flat paint, no 3D objects.
