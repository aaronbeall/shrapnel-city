SHEET THEME: LIGHT FIXTURES SEEN STRAIGHT FROM DIRECTLY ABOVE (top-down orthographic, looking straight down at the floor or wall top they are mounted on), SWITCHED ON AND GLOWING. Absolutely no side views, no perspective, no domes or beacons seen from the side, no cylinders standing up: every fixture is a flat shape as seen from overhead, like a lamp recessed into a ceiling photographed from below. Items: round recessed lamps, square and octagonal caged lamps, long rectangular strip lights, small round indicator lights, rows and grids of tiny LEDs set into a dark plate, ring lights. ONLY FOUR LIGHT COLOURS, nothing else: pure white, cool blue, very pale near-white yellow, and red. No green, no amber, no orange, no cyan, no purple, no screens. Roughly equal numbers of each colour. Bright glowing cores with a soft bloom kept inside each item; dark or grey metal housings.
