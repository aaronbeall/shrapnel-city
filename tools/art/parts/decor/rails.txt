SHEET THEME: LONG THIN RAILINGS, PIPES AND CONDUITS seen straight from above. Items must be long and narrow (much longer than wide): straight safety railing sections with evenly spaced posts (several lengths, some yellow-painted), railing corners, single pipes and bundles of 2 to 4 parallel pipes in several lengths, pipe elbows and T-junctions, a pipe with a wheel valve, pipe brackets, flexible ribbed conduits, cable trays carrying coloured cables, narrow catwalk grating strips.
