SHEET THEME: SMALL MARKINGS AND FITTINGS ON THE TOP OF A LIGHT GREY METAL WALL. Each item is drawn on its own small rectangular patch of light grey wall plating (the patch is part of the item): yellow-black warning stripe plates, red danger chevrons, stencilled numbers, a bolted repair patch, a small hatch, a keypad, a rust stain, a scorch mark, a row of rivets, a cable clip with a short cable, an access panel with screws, a red emergency handle. Mostly flat with very shallow relief.
